1.  **The Head Fake (1914):** The expectation of the *Schlacht* (decisive battle) vs. the reality of the *Materialschlacht* (war of materiel/attrition). The "wrong turn in Sarajevo" vs. the "one-way tickets" of mobilization timetables.
