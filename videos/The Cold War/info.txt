# Comprehensive Historical Research: The Cold War (1947–1991)

---

## 1. BACKGROUND

### 1.1 Historical Context
*   **Power Vacuum (1945):** The defeat of Nazi Germany and Imperial Japan left a massive power vacuum in Europe and Asia. The traditional European great powers (UK, France, Germany) were exhausted, bankrupt, or destroyed.
*   **Bipolarity:** Only two powers possessed the industrial capacity, military reach, and ideological drive to fill the vacuum: the **United States** and the **Union of Soviet Socialist Republics (USSR)**.
*   **Ideological Incompatibility:** The conflict was structural, not merely personal.
    *   **USA:** Liberal democracy, free-market capitalism, individual liberty, self-determination (Wilsonian idealism).
    *   **USSR:** Marxist-Leninism, command economy, single-party state (vanguard party), world revolution (initially), "Socialism in One Country" (Stalinist modification), security buffer zones.
*   **Wartime Alliance Fractures:** The Grand Alliance (US, UK, USSR) was a "marriage of convenience" against Hitler. Mutual suspicion predated 1945 (Western intervention in Russian Civil War 1918–1920; Nazi-Soviet Pact 1939–1941).

### 1.2 Political Situation (Immediate Post-War)
*   **Yalta (Feb 1945) & Potsdam (Jul–Aug 1945) Conferences:** Agreements on German occupation zones, UN formation, and Soviet entry into Pacific War. **Key Dispute:** "Free elections" in Eastern Europe (Stalin wanted friendly governments; Roosevelt/Churchill wanted democratic ones).
*   **United Nations Formation (Oct 1945):** Created as a forum for great power cooperation (Security Council veto power enshrined great power privilege), but immediately paralyzed by US-USSR vetoes.
*   **Decolonization:** European empires collapsing (India 1947, Indonesia, French Indochina, Africa). Created "Third World" battleground for influence.

### 1.3 Economic Situation
*   **USA:** Emerged from WWII with ~50% of world GDP, sole nuclear power (until 1949), industrial base intact, creditor nation. **Goal:** Open markets, free trade (Bretton Woods system: IMF, World Bank, GATT), prevent return of Great Depression.
*   **USSR:** Devastated (~27 million dead, 1,710 towns, 70,000 villages destroyed). Heavy industry relocated to Urals survived. **Goal:** Reparations from Germany, closed economic bloc (Comecon established 1949), rapid militarized industrialization (Five-Year Plans).
*   **Europe:** Economic collapse, food shortages, communist parties strong in France/Italy (risk of electoral communist victories).

### 1.4 Social Situation
*   **Displacement:** ~40 million refugees/displaced persons in Europe (expulsions of Germans from East, Poles from Kresy, Holocaust survivors).
*   **War Weariness vs. Vigilance:** US public desired demobilization; Truman faced "return to normalcy" pressure. USSR society mobilized for total reconstruction under totalitarian control.
*   **Ideological Appeal:** Communism retained prestige for defeating fascism and offering anti-colonial framework; US democracy offered consumer prosperity (eventually).

### 1.5 Religious Situation
*   **State Atheism (USSR):** Systematic persecution of Russian Orthodox Church, Catholic Church (Ukraine, Lithuania), Islam (Central Asia), Judaism. Religion framed as "opium of the people" and tool of bourgeois nationalism.
*   **Civil Religion (USA):** "In God We Trust" (motto 1956), "Under God" added to Pledge (1954). Framed Cold War as spiritual battle: "Godless Communism" vs. Judeo-Christian Civilization.
*   **Vatican:** Pope Pius XII fervently anti-communist; later Ostpolitik (Paul VI) sought dialogue with Eastern Bloc.

## 2. TIMELINE: KEY DATES & CHRONOLOGY

### Phase I: Origins & Consolidation (1945–1953)
*   **1945 Feb:** Yalta Conference.
*   **1945 Jul–Aug:** Potsdam Conference; Atomic bombs dropped on Hiroshima/Nagasaki (Aug 6/9).
*   **1946 Feb:** Stalin's "Two Camps" speech (inevitability of war with capitalism).
*   **1946 Mar:** Churchill "Iron Curtain" speech (Fulton, Missouri).
*   **1946–47:** Greek Civil War intensifies; British inform US they can no longer support Greece/Turkey.
*   **1947 Mar 12:** **Truman Doctrine** announced (containment policy).
*   **1947 Jun:** **Marshall Plan** (European Recovery Program) announced.
*   **1947 Sept:** Cominform founded (Soviet control of European communist parties).
*   **1948 Feb:** Communist coup in Czechoslovakia.
*   **1948 Jun–1949 May:** **Berlin Blockade & Airlift**.
*   **1949 Apr:** **NATO** founded (Washington Treaty).
*   **1949 Aug:** USSR tests first atomic bomb (RDS-1 "Joe-1").
*   **1949 Oct:** **People's Republic of China (PRC)** proclaimed (Mao Zedong).
*   **1950 Feb:** Sino-Soviet Treaty of Friendship.
*   **1950 Jun 25:** **Korean War begins** (North invades South).
*   **1951 Apr:** MacArthur relieved of command.
*   **1952:** US tests first thermonuclear device (Ivy Mike); UK tests atomic bomb.
*   **1953 Mar:** Stalin dies.
*   **1953 Jul:** Korean Armistice signed.

### Phase II: "Peaceful Coexistence" & Crises (1953–1962)
*   **1954:** Geneva Conference (Indochina partitioned; Vietnam temporary divide at 17th parallel).
*   **1955 May:** **Warsaw Pact** founded (response to West German NATO entry).
*   **1955 Jul:** Geneva Summit (first since Potsdam; "Spirit of Geneva").
*   **1956 Feb:** Khrushchev's "Secret Speech" (20th Party Congress, denouncing Stalin).
*   **1956 Jun:** Poznań protests (Poland).
*   **1956 Oct–Nov:** **Hungarian Revolution** crushed by Soviet tanks; **Suez Crisis** (UK/France/Israel vs Egypt – US opposes allies).
*   **1957 Oct:** **Sputnik 1** launched (Space Race begins).
*   **1958:** Second Taiwan Strait Crisis; Khrushchev Berlin Ultimatum (6 months).
*   **1959:** Cuban Revolution succeeds (Castro); Khrushchev visits USA (Camp David).
*   **1960 May:** U-2 Incident (Gary Powers shot down); Paris Summit collapses.
*   **1961 Apr:** **Bay of Pigs Invasion** fails.
*   **1961 Aug:** **Berlin Wall** construction begins.
*   **1961 Oct:** Tsar Bomba tested (50 Mt, largest ever).
*   **1962 Oct:** **Cuban Missile Crisis** (13 days, brink of nuclear war).

### Phase III: Détente & Proxy Wars (1963–1979)
*   **1963 Jun:** "Hotline" established; **Partial Nuclear Test Ban Treaty** signed.
*   **1964 Oct:** Khrushchev ousted; Brezhnev/Kosygin take power.
*   **1964 Aug:** Gulf of Tonkin Incident → **Vietnam War** major US escalation.
*   **1966–76:** **Cultural Revolution** (China) – chaos, Sino-Soviet border clashes (1969).
*   **1968 Jan:** Prague Spring begins (Dubček).
*   **1968 Aug:** Warsaw Pact invasion of Czechoslovakia (Brezhnev Doctrine).
*   **1968:** Tet Offensive (Vietnam); Global student protests.
*   **1969:** Nixon Doctrine (Vietnamization); SALT I talks begin (Helsinki/Vienna).
*   **1971:** Ping Pong Diplomacy; Kissinger secret visit to Beijing; PRC gets UN seat (Taiwan expelled).
*   **1972 Feb:** **Nixon visits China** (Shanghai Communiqué).
*   **1972 May:** **Nixon visits Moscow** (SALT I, ABM Treaty, Basic Principles Agreement).
*   **1973:** Paris Peace Accords (US exits Vietnam); Yom Kippur War (nuclear alert DEFCON 3); Oil Crisis.
*   **1975 Apr:** Fall of Saigon / Phnom Penh.
*   **1975 Aug:** **Helsinki Accords** (CSCE) – Borders, Human Rights, Cooperation.
*   **1977:** Carter Presidency – Human Rights emphasis; Neutron Bomb controversy.
*   **1979 Jan:** US-PRC full diplomatic recognition.
*   **1979 Jun:** SALT II signed (never ratified).
*   **1979 Nov:** Iranian Revolution / Hostage Crisis.
*   **1979 Dec:** **Soviet Invasion of Afghanistan** → End of Détente.

### Phase IV: "Second Cold War" & Escalation (1980–1985)
*   **1980:** Solidarity movement founded in Poland (Lech Wałęsa); Carter Doctrine (Gulf); Olympic boycotts (Moscow 1980 / LA 1984).
*   **1981:** Reagan inaugurated; Martial Law in Poland (Jaruzelski, Dec).
*   **1982:** Falklands War (US supports UK); Brezhnev dies (Nov) → Andropov.
*   **1983:** KAL 007 shot down; Able Archer 83 (NATO exercise misinterpreted as first strike prep); Reagan "Evil Empire" speech; SDI ("Star Wars") announced; US invades Grenada.
*   **1984:** Chernenko succeeds Andropov.
*   **1985 Mar:** **Gorbachev** becomes General Secretary.

### Phase V: Endgame & Collapse (1985–1991)
*   **1985 Nov:** Geneva Summit (Reagan-Gorbachev).
*   **1986 Apr:** Chernobyl Disaster (accelerates *Glasnost*).
*   **1986 Oct:** Reykjavík Summit (near deal on abolition of nukes, fails on SDI).
*   **1987 Dec:** **INF Treaty** signed (eliminates intermediate-range nukes in Europe).
*   **1988:** Gorbachev UN Speech (renounces Brezhnev Doctrine, "Freedom of Choice"); Soviet withdrawal from Afghanistan begins (ends Feb 1989).
*   **1989 Jun:** Polish Round Table → Semi-free elections (Solidarity wins); Tiananmen Square Massacre (China).
*   **1989 Aug:** Pan-European Picnic (Hungary opens border).
*   **1989 Nov 9:** **Fall of the Berlin Wall**.
*   **1989 Nov–Dec:** Velvet Revolution (Czechoslovakia); Romanian Revolution (Ceaușescu executed Dec 25).
*   **1990 Mar:** Lithuanian independence declared.
*   **1990 Oct:** **German Reunification** (Two Plus Four Treaty).
*   **1991 Jul:** **Warsaw Pact dissolved**.
*   **1991 Aug:** **August Coup** attempt against Gorbachev (fails; Yeltsin rises).
*   **1991 Dec 8:** Belavezha Accords (Russia, Ukraine, Belarus dissolve USSR, create CIS).
*   **1991 Dec 25:** Gorbachev resigns; Soviet flag lowered from Kremlin.
*   **1991 Dec 26:** Supreme Soviet votes USSR out of existence.

## 5. MAJOR EVENTS (DETAILED ANALYSIS)

### 5.1 The Greek Civil War & Truman Doctrine (1947)
*   **What:** British withdrawal from supporting Greek monarchists vs. Communist guerrillas (KKE/DSE). Truman asks Congress for $400M aid for Greece/Turkey.
*   **Why:** UK bankruptcy; Soviet pressure on Turkey (Straits); Fear of "domino effect" in Mediterranean.
*   **Who:** Truman, Acheson, British FM Bevin, Greek King/Generals, KKE (backed by Yugoslavia/Albania/Bulgaria - *not* Stalin initially).
*   **Immediate:** US assumes global policeman role; Greece/Turkey stay Western.
*   **Long-term:** **Containment Doctrine** formalized; NSC-68 militarization; Precedent for intervention in civil wars globally.

### 5.2 The Marshall Plan (European Recovery Program) (1948–1952)
*   **What:** $13.3 Billion (approx $150B today) grants/loans to 16 European nations. Administered by OEEC (precursor OECD).
*   **Why:** Prevent economic collapse -> Communist electoral victory (France/Italy); Create US export markets; Integrate West German economy.
*   **Who:** SecState George Marshall, Acheson, Paul Hoffman (ECA), European leaders (Bidault, Bevin, De Gasperi, Adenauer).
*   **USSR Reaction:** Molotov walks out Paris talks (Jul 1947); Forces Eastern Bloc rejection; Creates **Cominform** (Sept 1947) to enforce discipline.
*   **Consequences:** Western Europe GDP surpasses pre-war by 1951; Political stability; Foundation of EU (ECSC 1951); Deepens East/West split.

### 5.3 Berlin Blockade & Airlift (Jun 1948 – May 1949)
*   **What:** Soviets block rail/road/canal access to West Berlin (2.2M people) over currency reform (Deutsche Mark). US/UK launch **Operation Vittles** / **Plainfare**.
*   **Scale:** 278,000 flights; 2.3M tons supplies (coal, flour, candy "Operation Little Vittles"); 1 plane/30 sec at peak; 31 US/39 UK/8 German fatalities.
*   **Why:** Stalin tests Western resolve; Tries to force West out of Berlin; Fails due to air superiority & German cooperation.
*   **Consequences:** **NATO formation (Apr 1949)**; **FRG founded (May 1949)**; **GDR founded (Oct 1949)**; Germany permanently divided; US commitment to Europe irreversible.

### 5.4 The "Loss of China" & Korean War (1949–1953)
*   **China 1949:** Mao wins Civil War. US "China Lobby" blames Truman. NSC-68 (Apr 1950) calls for massive rearmament.
*   **Korea Jun 1950:** Kim Il-sung invades South (Stalin approves, Mao reluctant). UNSC Res 83/84 (USSR boycotting) authorizes force.
*   **Phases:**
    1.  **Pusan Perimeter** (Aug 1950) - US/ROK hold.
    2.  **Inchon Landing** (Sep 1950) - MacArthur amphibious envelopment.
    3.  **Drive to Yalu** (Oct 1950) - UN crosses 38th Parallel (Rollback).
    4.  **Chinese Intervention** (Nov 1950) - "Volunteers" push UN back (Chosin Reservoir).
    5.  **Stalemate** (1951–53) - Static trench warfare near 38th Parallel.
*   **MacArthur Firing (Apr 1951):** Dispute over widening war (bomb China, blockade, Kuomintang). Civilian control affirmed.
*   **Armistice (Jul 1953):** DMZ created. No peace treaty.
*   **Casualties:** ~2.5M Korean civilians, ~1.2M Chinese/NK, ~36k US, ~3k UN others.
*   **Impact:** **Militarized Containment**; NATO buildup; US-Japan Security Treaty; Rearmament of West Germany; US defense budget quadruples; Taiwan secured (7th Fleet).

### 5.5 Death of Stalin & The Thaw (1953–1956)
*   **Power Struggle:** Beria (executed), Malenkov, Molotov vs. Khrushchev (Party control).
*   **20th Party Congress (Feb 1956):** **Secret Speech** ("On the Cult of Personality"). Denounces Stalin's purges, deportations, wartime errors.
*   **Impact:** Shock in Communist world; Riots in Georgia; **Poznań (Poland Jun '56)**; **Hungarian Revolution (Oct–Nov '56)**.
*   **Hungary 1956:** Students -> Workers -> National Govt (Nagy) -> Withdrawal from Warsaw Pact / Neutrality. **Soviet Invasion (Nov 4):** "Operation Whirlwind." ~2,500 Hungarians, ~700 Soviets killed. 200k refugees. **Kádár** installed.
*   **Significance:** Limits of "Peaceful Coexistence" defined; USSR will use force to hold Empire; Western "Rollback" rhetoric exposed as hollow (Eisenhower did not intervene).

### 5.6 Suez Crisis (1956) – The West Fractures
*   **Trigger:** Nasser nationalizes Suez Canal Co. (Jul).
*   **Protocol of Sèvres (Oct):** Secret UK/France/Israel collusion. Israel invades Sinai; UK/France ultimatum -> Bombing -> Invasion.
*   **US Reaction:** **Eisenhower furious.** Financial pressure on UK (Sterling crisis); UN condemnation; **US/USSR rare alignment** at UN.
*   **Result:** Anglo-French withdrawal (Dec); **Eden resigns**; **US replaces UK/France as dominant Western power in Middle East** (Eisenhower Doctrine 1957); Nasser triumphs; USSR gains prestige/foothold in Egypt/Syria.

### 5.7 Sputnik & The Missile Gap (1957–1960)
*   **Oct 4, 1957:** **Sputnik 1** (83.6 kg, beeping sphere). **Nov 3:** Sputnik 2 (Laika dog).
*   **US Panic:** "Pearl Harbor of Science"; Education reform (NDEA 1958); NASA created (1958); DARPA created.
*   **Missile Gap:** Gaither Report / Kennedy campaign claims USSR ahead in ICBMs. **Reality (1961):** US had overwhelming superiority (Atlas, Titan, Polaris, B-52s). Khrushchev bluffing with few R-7s (liquid fuel, vulnerable).
*   **Impact:** Space Race; ICBM focus over bombers; Nuclear triad solidified.

### 5.8 Berlin Crisis & The Wall (1958–1961)
*   **Khrushchev Ultimatum (Nov 1958):** 6 months to make West Berlin "Free City" or sign peace treaty with GDR (handing access control to Ulbricht).
*   **Summits:** Geneva (1959), Camp David (1959 - "Spirit"), Paris (1960 - **U-2 Shootdown** ruins it), Vienna (Jun 1961 - Kennedy bullied).
*   **Refugee Crisis:** 3.5M East Germans fled 1949–61 (brain drain). 1,000+/day summer 1961.
*   **Aug 13, 1961:** **"Operation Rose"** - Barbed wire -> Concrete Wall (155 km). **Tanks face off at Checkpoint Charlie (Oct).**
*   **Result:** Stemmed bleeding; Stabilized GDR; **Kennedy:** "A wall is a hell of a lot better than a war." Propaganda defeat for Communism (wall keeps people *in*).

### 5.9 Cuban Missile Crisis (Oct 16–28, 1962) – **The Pivot Point**
*   **Origins:** US Jupiter missiles in Turkey/Italy (1961); Bay of Pigs failure; Castro fears invasion; Khrushchev seeks cheap parity / defend ally.
*   **Deployment (Summer 1962):** **Operation Anadyr.** 42,000 Soviet troops (disguised), **R-12 (SS-4) / R-14 (SS-5) MRBMs/IRBMs** (range 2,000/4,500 km), **IL-28 bombers**, **Luna (FROG) tactical nukes** (98 tactical warheads - *unknown to US at time*), SA-2 SAMs.
*   **Discovery:** U-2 photos (Oct 14) analyzed Oct 15. **EXCOMM** formed (Oct 16).
*   **Options:** Air strike -> Invasion -> Blockade/Quarantine -> Diplomacy.
*   **Decision:** **Naval Quarantine** (Oct 22 TV address). DEFCON 2 (SAC at DEFCON 2, rest DEFCON 3).
*   **Critical Moments:**
    *   Oct 24: Soviet ships stop/turn back.
    *   Oct 26: **"Long Telegram" (Khrushchev to JFK):** Remove missiles for no-invasion pledge.
    *   Oct 27: **"Black Saturday":** U-2 (Rudolf Anderson) shot down over Cuba (SAM); U-2 (Maultsby) strays into USSR airspace (MiGs scramble); **Second Khrushchev letter:** Demands Jupiter removal from Turkey.
    *   **Resolution:** RFK/Dobrynin **Secret Deal:** Public: US pledges no invasion + UN verification. **Secret:** Jupiters removed from Turkey within 6 months (done Apr 1963).
*   **Consequences:** **Hotline** (Jun 1963); **Partial Test Ban Treaty** (Aug 1963); Khrushchev ousted (1964); **Mutual Assured Destruction (MAD)** accepted as reality; Shift to **Détente/Arms Control**.

### 5.10 Vietnam War (US Phase: 1964–1973) – *See Military Section for Battles*
*   **Gulf of Tonkin (Aug 1964):** Dubious attacks -> Resolution (blank check).
*   **Escalation:** Operation Rolling Thunder (bombing North); 500k+ US troops (1968).
*   **Tet Offensive (Jan/Feb 1968):** Military defeat for VC/NVA; **Political/Strategic Victory** - broke US will. Cronkite: "Mired in stalemate." Johnson drops out.
*   **Nixon/Kissinger:** Vietnamization; Cambodia/Laos incursions; Christmas Bombing (Dec 1972); **Paris Peace Accords (Jan 1973)**.
*   **Fall of Saigon (Apr 30, 1975):** NVA tanks breach Independence Palace. Helicopter evacuation (Frequent Wind).
*   **Cost:** ~58,220 US dead; ~1.1M NVA/VC; ~250k ARVN; ~2M civilians. $168B (1970s $). **War Powers Act (1973);** "Vietnam Syndrome" restrains US intervention.

### 5.11 Prague Spring & Brezhnev Doctrine (1968)
*   **Dubček:** "Socialism with a human face" (censorship lifted, federalization, travel).
*   **Warsaw Pact Invasion (Aug 20-21):** ~250k troops (USSR, Poland, Hungary, Bulgaria, GDR - Romania/Albania refuse). **Operation Danube.**
*   **Resistance:** Non-violent (street signs removed, radio free); Jan Palach self-immolation (Jan '69).
*   **Brezhnev Doctrine:** "When forces hostile to socialism try to turn development of some socialist country towards capitalism, it becomes not only a problem of the country concerned, but a common problem of all socialist countries." **Limited Sovereignty.**
*   **Impact:** Eurocommunism splits from Moscow; Romania/Albania drift; China condemns "Social Imperialism"; Détente continues (realpolitik) but ideological freeze in East.

### 5.12 Détente: High Points (1969–1975)
*   **SALT I (May 1972):** **Interim Agreement** (Freezes ICBM launchers at existing levels: US 1054, USSR 1618 - *disparity favors USSR heavy missiles*); **ABM Treaty** (Limits to 2 sites each -> later 1: Grand Forks/Moscow). **Crucial:** MAD codified in law.
*   **Nixon in China (Feb 1972):** **Shanghai Communiqué.** "One China" (Taiwan is part of China); Anti-hegemony clause (anti-USSR). **Triangular Diplomacy** works.
*   **Nixon in Moscow (May 1972):** Basic Principles Agreement (Rules of road: no nuclear war, peaceful coexistence, mutual restraint).
*   **Helsinki Final Act (Aug 1975):** **CSCE (35 nations).** **Basket I:** Borders inviolable (USSR win). **Basket II:** Economic/Scientific cooperation. **Basket III:** **Human Rights** (Freedoms of thought, conscience, religion, movement - *Western win, long-term time bomb for USSR*).
*   **Trade:** Grain deals (1972, 1975); US credits; Pepsi-Cola in USSR.

### 5.13 Soviet Invasion of Afghanistan (Dec 1979 – Feb 1989)
*   **Why:** Communist coup (Saour Rev '78) -> Infighting (Khalq vs Parcham) -> Insurgency (Mujahideen) -> Amin's unreliability -> **KGB assassinates Amin (Dec 27, Op Storm-333)** -> Install **Karmal**.
*   **US Response (Carter -> Reagan):** **Carter Doctrine** (Gulf security); Grain embargo; Olympic Boycott (Moscow 1980); **CIA/ISI (Pakistan) + Saudi funding** -> **Operation Cyclone** (Stingers 1986).
*   **War:** 100k-150k Soviet troops (peak); **Guerrilla war**; Mines, scorched earth, refugee crisis (6M to Pak/Iran).
*   **Cost:** ~15k Soviet dead; 50k+ wounded; ~1M Afghan dead; $50B+ cost; **"Soviet Vietnam."**
*   **Withdrawal:** **Geneva Accords (Apr 1988)**; Last troop leaves Feb 15, 1989. **Najibullah survives until 1992.**

### 5.14 The "Second Cold War" (1979–1985)
*   **Euromissile Crisis:** NATO **Dual Track Decision (Dec 1979)**: Deploy 572 Pershing II / GLCM (Ground Launched Cruise Missiles) in W. Europe from 1983 *unless* USSR removes SS-20s. Massive peace movements (CND, Greenham Common, 500k+ protests).
*   **Reagan Buildup:** FY1981-85 budgets +35% real; **600-ship Navy**; B-1B; MX Missile (Peacekeeper); **SDI (Strategic Defense Initiative - Mar 1983)**.
*   **Rhetoric:** "Evil Empire" (Mar 1983); "Ash heap of history."
*   **KAL 007 (Sep 1983):** Soviet SU-15 shoots down 747 (269 dead, Cong. Larry McDonald). Reagan: "Barbarism."
*   **Able Archer 83 (Nov 1983):** NATO CPX (Command Post Exercise) simulating nuclear release. **Soviet RYAN intelligence program** detects "preparation for surprise attack." **Closest to accidental nuclear war since 1962.** Gorbachev later cites this as wake-up call.

### 5.15 The Gorbachev Revolution & Endgame (1985–1991)
*   **Perestroika (Restructuring):** Economic: Law on State Enterprise (1987), Cooperatives (1988), Joint Ventures. Political: Contested elections (1989 Congress of People's Deputies), Presidency (1990).
*   **Glasnost (Openness):** Media freedom; History revealed (Stalin crimes, Katyn, Molotov-Ribbentrop secret protocol); **Chernobyl (Apr 1986)** catalyst.
*   **New Thinking (Foreign Policy):** **Common Security** > Class Struggle; **Sinatra Doctrine** ("They do it their way") replaces Brezhnev Doctrine; **Unilateral force cuts** (UN Dec 1988).
*   **Summits:**
    *   **Geneva (Nov 1985):** First meeting; "Nuclear war cannot be won."
    *   **Reykjavík (Oct 1986):** **Near-total abolition deal** (zero INF, 50% strategic, zero in 10 yrs). **Collapses on SDI** (Reagan: lab research only; Gorbachev: treaty ban). *Breakthrough on INF.*
    *   **Washington (Dec 1987):** **INF Treaty Signed.** Eliminates **all** ground-launched ballistic/cruise missiles 500-5,500 km (US: 846, USSR: 1,846). **On-site verification.**
    *   **Moscow (May/Jun 1988):** START framework; Human rights focus.
    *   **Malta (Dec 1989):** Bush/Gorbachev: "Cold War dumped in Mediterranean."
*   **Eastern Europe 1989 (The Autumn of Nations):**
    *   **Poland:** Round Table (Feb–Apr) -> Semi-free elections (Jun 4) -> **Solidarity landslide** -> **Mazowiecki PM (Aug)** - First non-Communist govt.
    *   **Hungary:** Border fence cut (May); **Pan-European Picnic (Aug 19)**; Border open to Austria (Sep 11) -> Exodus of E. Germans.
    *   **East Germany:** Monday Demos (Leipzig); Honecker ousted (Oct 18); **Wall Opened (Nov 9)** - Schabowski press conference error.
    *   **Czechoslovakia:** Velvet Revolution (Nov 17–Dec 29) -> Havel President.
    *   **Romania:** Timișoara (Dec 16) -> Ceaușescu flee/executed (Dec 25).
    *   **Bulgaria:** Zhivkov ousted (Nov 10).
*   **German Reunification (1990):** **Two Plus Four Treaty** (Sep 12): FRG, GDR, US, USSR, UK, France. **Full sovereignty; NATO membership; Borders confirmed (Oder-Neisse).** Oct 3: Official Unity Day.
*   **USSR Dissolution:**
    *   **Mar 1991 Referendum:** 76% vote for renewed federation (Boycotted by Baltics, Georgia, Armenia, Moldova).
    *   **Aug 1991 Coup (GKChP):** Hardliners (Yanayev, Yazov, Pugo, Kryuchkov) detain Gorbachev (Foros). **Yeltsin** on tank (White House/Russian Parliament) defies coup. Collapses in 3 days.
    *   **Belavezha Accords (Dec 8, Belarus):** Yeltsin, Kravchuk (Ukraine), Shushkevich (Belarus) sign: **USSR ceases to exist; CIS formed.**
    *   **Alma-Ata Protocol (Dec 21):** 11 republics join CIS.
    *   **Dec 25, 1991:** Gorbachev resigns. **Soviet Flag lowered; Russian Tricolor raised.**
    *   **Dec 26:** Soviet of Republics votes dissolution.

### 7.2 Major Treaties & Agreements (Chronological)
1.  **Antarctic Treaty (1959):** Demilitarized, scientific preserve. First Cold War arms control.
2.  **Partial Test Ban Treaty (PTBT) (1963):** Bans atmospheric, underwater, space tests. *Underground allowed.* Signed US, UK, USSR. (France/China refuse).
3.  **Outer Space Treaty (1967):** No WMD in orbit/celestial bodies; Moon peaceful use.
4.  **Treaty of Tlatelolco (1967):** Latin America Nuclear-Weapon-Free Zone.
5.  **Nuclear Non-Proliferation Treaty (NPT) (1968/1970):** **Grand Bargain:** Non-nuclear states forswear nukes -> Access to peaceful nuclear tech; Nuclear states (P5) pursue disarmament (Art VI). *Israel, India, Pakistan, N. Korea (later) stay out.*
6.  **Seabed Treaty (1971):** No nukes on ocean floor.
7.  **SALT I (1972):** Interim Agreement (Launchers) + ABM Treaty.
8.  **ABM Treaty (1972):** 1 site each (National Capital / ICBM field). **Cornerstone of MAD.** US withdraws 2002 (Bush).
9.  **Basic Principles of Relations (1972):** Rules of the Road.
10. **SALT II (1979):** Signed, not ratified (Afghanistan). Limits launchers (2,250), MIRVed ICBMs. **Observed voluntarily until 1986.**
11. **INF Treaty (1987):** **Zero-Zero** option (Global ban 500-5,500 km). **Intrusive Verification** (On-site, Portal monitoring at Votkinsk/Magna). *US withdraws 2019 (Trump) citing Russian 9M729 violation.*
12. **START I (1991):** **Strategic Arms Reduction Treaty.** 6,000 warheads / 1,600 delivery vehicles. Deep reductions. Entered force Dec 1994. Implemented by successor states (Lisbon Protocol 1992).
13. **CFE Treaty (1990):** Conventional Armed Forces in Europe. Atlantic to Urals. **Destruction verified** (Tanks, Arty, ACVs, Helis, Aircraft). **Adapted 1999; Russia suspends 2007; NATO suspends 2011.**
